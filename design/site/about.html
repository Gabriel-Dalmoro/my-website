Hi, I'm Gabriel.

This site is part portfolio, part journal: entries from my notebooks over the years, the work I'm proud of, and a place to make things just because I want to. Make yourself at home.

mul·ti·po·ten·tial·itenoun
A person with many interests and many crafts. See also: me.

I was born and raised in Brazil, grew up for 13 years in Canada, and now live in France.

I'm a builder by trade. Three years as a full-stack developer, now automations, websites and my first SaaS.

I'm an explorer the rest of the time. Peaks in the Rockies and the Andes, waves in Peru and Brazil, snow in the Alps.

And I'm a forever student. Of languages, of people, of whatever I haven't tried yet.
