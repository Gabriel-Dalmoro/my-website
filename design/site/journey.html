    Brasil · born and raisedChapter 01

    It all started in Brazil

    I was born and raised in Brazil. My mom gave me music, and there has been a guitar within reach ever since.

    Canada · 13 years51.4°N 116.2°W

    Then came the mountains

    We moved to Calgary, and for 13 years the Rockies were my weekend plan. If there was a peak, I wanted to stand on it.

    Canada · winterChapter 03

    Winters meant snowboarding

    Cold lifts, short days, first chair whenever I could get it. I still ride every winter, now in the Alps.

    Calgary · every dayChapter 04

    My dad gave me training

    He's a personal trainer, so moving every day was never optional in our house. Today I'm also helping him launch FITtoExcel, his fitness-tech startup.

    Peru → Brazil7.7°S 79.4°W

    Back to the sea

    In 2024 I left Canada with my best friend and a very heavy backpack. After half my life far from the ocean, I went looking for waves. I rode the longest wave in the world in Chicama, Peru. My biggest one yet: 2.3 m, in Florianópolis.

    Cordillera Huayhuash, Peru10.3°S 76.9°W

    Then I went up

    We trekked for days through the Huayhuash, above 4,000 m, past turquoise lakes. Day one topped out at 4,265 m. I wrote it down, obviously.

    Peru · Bolivia · Chile13.2°S 72.5°W

    Every place taught me something

    Walking through walls built over 500 years ago. Then salt flats in Bolivia, the Atacama desert, salsa classes and Spanish homework in La Paz.

    Now · FranceChapter 08

    Now I build from France

    Weekends in the Alps, weekdays at the desk. Brazil, Canada and the whole road come with me.
