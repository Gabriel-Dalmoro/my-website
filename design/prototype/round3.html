    Peru → Brazil7.7°S 79.4°W

    Back to the sea

    In 2024 I left Canada with my best friend and a very heavy backpack. After half my life far from the ocean, I went looking for waves. I rode the longest wave in the world in Chicama, Peru. My biggest one yet: 2.3 m, in Florianópolis.

    Cordillera Huayhuash, Peru10.3°S 76.9°W

    Then I went up

    We trekked for days through the Huayhuash, above 4,000 m, past turquoise lakes. Day one topped out at 4,265 m. I wrote it down, obviously.

    Peru · Bolivia · Chile13.2°S 72.5°W

    Walking through walls built over 500 years ago. Then salt flats in Bolivia, the Atacama desert, salsa classes and Spanish homework in La Paz.
