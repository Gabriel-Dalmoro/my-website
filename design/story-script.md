# Story page: script (draft 1)

What the visitor sees, in order, from top to bottom.
Words in **[BRACKETS]** are facts I still need from you.

Legend:
- 🎬 **3D**: what happens in the scene
- 🕺 **Animation**: the Mixamo clip little you plays
- 📦 **Lands**: props that leave the orbit and settle on the pedestal
- 📍 **Pin**: language pin added to the pedestal rim
- ✍️ **Text**: what's written on the page
- 🖼 **Photo**: the real photo in the text card
- ↪ **Transition**: how we move to the next scene

---

## Scene 0: Opening — "What I'm made of"

🎬 **3D:** Little you floats in the middle of the screen, slowly turning. Around him, a calm,
loose orbit of 12 things: guitar, chimarrão, diploma, laptop, kettlebell, backpack,
surfboard, snowboard, desk lamp, notebook, and the language pins (🇧🇷 🇨🇦 🇪🇸 🇫🇷 🇩🇪 🇮🇹).
No pedestal yet: just soft paper background and a faint shadow below him.

🕺 **Animation:** *Idle* (floating, breathing). Arms relaxed.

✍️ **Text** (left side on desktop, under him on phone):

> **What I'm made of.**
>
> I'm Gabriel. I build automations, websites and software for businesses.
> But the way I work comes from everything that happened before.
> Here are the pieces, and how they came together.

Small hint at the bottom: *Scroll ↓*

↪ **Transition:** as the visitor starts scrolling, a stone pedestal rises from below.
Little you drifts down and lands on it (*Jumping*, landing part only).

## Scene 5: Now — Everything together

🎬 **3D:** The orbit is now empty. Everything sits together around his desk on the pedestal:
guitar, chimarrão, diploma, laptop and iPad, kettlebell, backpack, surfboard, snowboard.
The pins form a full ring on the rim, with 🇮🇹 IT left empty ("next").
Backdrop turns brand green. This is the integrated "final picture".

🕺 **Animation:** *Typing* at the desk. When tapped: *Sitting Thumbs Up* plus a hello in a random language.

✍️ **Text:**

> **Chapter 5 · Now**
> ### All of it, at one desk.
> Today I run an automation studio for busy founders, I'm building software for private
> chefs with Elisa, and I'm helping my dad launch FITtoExcel in Canada.
> Business sense, engineering, four languages, and a habit of figuring things out.
> That's what you get when we work together.
